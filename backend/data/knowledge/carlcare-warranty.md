TECNO, Infinix and itel smartphones carry a 13-month warranty; accessories 6 months. Customers need the purchase receipt.
Carlcare service centres: Lagos – Computer Village, Ikeja; Kano – Beirut Road; Onitsha – New Market Road; Ibadan – Ring Road.
Screen damage and water damage are not covered by warranty.
